A **vector** records a magnitude and direction, or equivalently an ordered
list of coordinates. For example, $\mathbf{x}=(1,2)^\top$ is a vector in
$\mathbb{R}^2$.

$$
\|\mathbf{x}\|_2 = \sqrt{1^2+2^2} = \sqrt{5}
$$
